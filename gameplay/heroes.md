# Heroes & Archetypes

Heroes are the characters you play. Each has a unique kit of abilities, a visual identity (with collectible skins and avatars), and a combat **archetype** that defines its role and how it grows stronger.

## The five archetypes

Every hero belongs to one of five archetypes. The archetype determines the hero's role in a team and shapes the stat bonuses it gains as you upgrade it.

| Archetype | Role | Identity |
|-----------|------|----------|
| **Tank** | Frontline | High HP, armor, and sustain; soaks damage and protects allies. |
| **Fighter** | Bruiser | Balanced offense and durability; trades and brawls. |
| **Assassin** | Burst | Extreme damage, low defense; hunts priority targets. |
| **Marksman** | Ranged DPS | Highest sustained damage, most fragile; needs protection. |
| **Support** | Enabler | Healing-focused, low damage; keeps the team alive. |

## Star Ranks: how heroes grow

Each hero can be upgraded through **five star ranks** (1★ to 5★). Upgrading costs **Hero Shards** and **Gold**, and grants stat bonuses tuned to the hero's archetype. Progression is **capped at 5★** — there is no infinite power scaling, which keeps matches fair.

The stat bonuses accelerate at higher ranks. As a rough shape: 2★ grants about 13% of the full 5★ bonus, 3★ about a third, 4★ about 60%, and 5★ the full amount.

### Archetype stat bonuses at 5★

These are the full bonuses each archetype receives at maximum star rank, expressed as the approximate percentage gain over the hero's base stats:

| Archetype | HP | Attack | Armor | HP Regen | Design intent |
|-----------|----|--------|-------|----------|---------------|
| **Tank** | ~40% | ~25% | ~40% | ~40% | All-around tankiness |
| **Fighter** | ~33% | ~40% | ~33% | ~33% | Balanced growth |
| **Assassin** | ~22% | ~50% | ~20% | ~20% | Maximum burst |
| **Marksman** | ~19% | ~55% | ~15% | ~17% | Highest damage, most fragile |
| **Support** | ~29% | ~20% | ~30% | ~50% | Sustain-focused |

The exact per-rank values are defined in the game's hero archetype configuration, with each of the 5 archetypes having tuned bonuses across all 5 star ranks.

## Hero Mastery

Beyond star ranks, each hero has a **Mastery** track (up to level 15) that rewards dedicated play on that specific hero. Mastery grants badges and exclusive rewards — including a special reward at the top rank (R15). Where star ranks make a hero stronger, mastery recognizes how well you *play* it. See [Progression Systems](progression.md).

## Heroes as collectibles

Heroes connect directly to the ownership economy:

- **Hero Cards** are NFTs that prove ownership of a hero and can be minted, collected, and traded.
- **Skins** change a hero's appearance and, at premium tiers, can be minted as tradeable NFTs.
- **Avatars** representing heroes serve as your profile identity.

For how these are minted and traded, see [NFT Assets](../nft-economy/nft-assets.md) and [Minting & Crafting](../nft-economy/minting.md).

## Putting it together

When you bring a hero into a match, its effective stats combine three things:

1. **Base stats** — the hero's intrinsic numbers.
2. **Archetype star-rank bonuses** — earned by upgrading with shards and gold (capped at 5★).
3. **In-match growth** — levels, items, and buffs gained during the game itself.

This layered design lets you invest in your favorite heroes over time while keeping the competitive ceiling fixed.
