# Items & Equipment

During a match, you spend earned gold on items that boost your stats, grant new effects, and shape your build. Outside of matches, related crafting systems turn duplicate materials into valuable cosmetics.

## In-match items

As you farm gold and experience, you buy items from the shop to power up your hero. Items can:

- Increase core stats (health, attack, defense, regeneration).
- Grant **active abilities** that occupy item skill slots.
- Apply **buffs** — passive bonuses that stack and combine with your hero's kit.

Items can be **merged** to build stronger versions, letting you progress from basic components to powerful completed equipment over the course of a match. The interface supports quick buying, merging, and selling so you can adapt your build to how the game is going.

## Buffs

Buffs are temporary or build-defining effects applied by items, abilities, and the environment. The buff system tracks:

- **Stacking** — how multiple sources combine.
- **Duration** — when effects tick and expire.
- **Triggers** — effects that fire on conditions (on hit, on damage taken, etc.).

Buffs from items are resolved by the same deterministic engine as combat, so their effects are consistent for everyone in the match.

## Inventory and the Bag

Outside of matches, your **Bag** holds the persistent items, materials, and cosmetics you've collected — the things that carry between games. This is distinct from the in-match item shop: the Bag is your long-term collection, including materials used for crafting NFTs.

## Crafting materials

Two key materials connect gameplay to the ownership economy:

| Material | Earned from | Used for |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Hero Shards** | Medal Road, matches, duplicate conversion | Upgrading hero star ranks (1★ → 5★) |
| **Skin Fragments** | Medal Road, matches, duplicate conversion | The Fragment Forge → premium / NFT skins |

When you receive a duplicate of something you already own, it can be **converted** into shards and gold rather than wasted — turning luck into steady progress.

## The Forge

The **Fragment Forge** is where skin fragments become finished skins, including premium and signature variants that can be minted as NFTs. Forging tiers scale in cost:

| Forge tier | Cost (Gold) | Extra |
|------------|-------------|-------|
| Rare | 5,000 | — |
| Epic | 15,000 | — |
| Legendary | 40,000 | — |
| Mythic | 80,000 | + Gems |

Signature skins are a special, high-quality result with fixed visual effects, crafted via dedicated recipes. For the full crafting-to-NFT flow, see [Minting & Crafting](../nft-economy/minting.md).

## Summary

- **In-match items** are temporary, bought with match gold, and define your build for that game.
- **The Bag** is your persistent collection across matches.
- **Materials** (shards and fragments) bridge gameplay and the economy, feeding hero upgrades and the Forge.
- **The Forge** turns materials into cosmetics, the best of which become tradeable NFTs.
