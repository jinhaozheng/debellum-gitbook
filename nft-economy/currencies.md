# Currencies

Debellum's economy runs on three off-chain soft currencies. Each has a clear purpose, and together they keep everyday play frictionless while the ownership layer anchors lasting value on-chain.

## The three currencies

| Currency | Type | Earned from | Spent on |
|----------|------|-------------|----------|
| **Gold** | Off-chain soft | Matches, Medal Road, Season Pass | Star ranks, Standard boxes, forging |
| **Gems** | Off-chain soft | Season Pass milestones, purchases | Premium/Legendary boxes, mythic forging |
| **Bellum Points (BP)** | Off-chain soft | Betting wins, playing matches | Bellum Box, BP Shop |

## Gold

Gold is the core soft currency, earned steadily through play. It funds the most common sinks: upgrading hero star ranks, opening Standard mystery boxes, and most forging tiers. Because it flows in from many sources (matches, the Medal Road, the Season Pass), Gold is the currency you'll interact with most.

### Gold sink examples

| Sink | Cost |
|------|------|
| Star Rank 2★ | 800 |
| Star Rank 3★ | 3,000 |
| Star Rank 4★ | 12,000 |
| Star Rank 5★ | 35,000 |
| Standard Box | 1,500 |

| Signature Skin | 30,000 |

## Gems

Gems are the premium soft currency, earned through Season Pass milestones and available via purchase. They gate higher-value content: Premium and Legendary mystery boxes, and the extra cost of mythic-tier forging.

| Sink | Cost |
|------|------|
| Premium Box | 50 |
| Legendary Box | 150 |
| Mythic Forge | +50 (on top of Gold) |

## Bellum Points (BP)

BP powers Debellum's betting and special-shop economy. You earn BP from betting wins and from playing matches, and spend it on the **Bellum Box** and **BP Shop**. BP is **server-authoritative** — the server is the source of truth for your balance after every betting or box action — which keeps the betting economy secure.

| Sink | Cost |
|------|------|
| Bellum Box | 2,000 |
| BP Shop items | Variable |
| Betting | Variable (50 – 1,000) |

## On-chain payment tokens

While the soft currencies above power day-to-day play, on-chain activity — buying and selling NFTs on the marketplace — settles in standard crypto tokens such as ETH, IMX, and USDT, depending on the chain and platform involved.

## Materials (not currencies, but close)

Two materials sit alongside the currencies and bridge gameplay to crafting:

| Material | Earned from | Used for |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Hero Shards** | Medal Road, matches, duplicate conversion | Hero star-rank upgrades |
| **Skin Fragments** | Medal Road, matches, duplicate conversion | The Fragment Forge → NFT skins |

Duplicates you already own are converted into shards and Gold rather than wasted.

## On-chain vs. off-chain, restated

The currency design embodies Debellum's core principle:

- **Gold, Gems, BP** stay **off-chain** — they change every match and must be instant and free.
- **NFT assets** are **on-chain** — they carry durable, tradeable value and power the ownership economy.

This is why you can play, earn, and spend soft currency without ever touching a blockchain transaction, while still participating in a real, owned economy when you choose to.
