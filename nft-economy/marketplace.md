# Marketplace

Once you own Debellum NFTs, you can trade them. The in-game marketplace aggregates listings across multiple major platforms, so you can buy, sell, and browse assets without leaving the game.

## A unified, multi-platform marketplace

Rather than locking you into a single venue, Debellum integrates with three established NFT marketplaces and surfaces them through one in-game interface:

| Platform | Role |
|----------|------|
| **OpenSea** | The largest general NFT marketplace. |
| **Element** | Multi-chain NFT marketplace. |
| **Immutable** | The native marketplace for Immutable zkEVM game assets. |

This means a listing you create in-game can reach buyers across these ecosystems, and you can discover assets listed on any of them from one place.

## What you can trade

The marketplace handles the tradeable NFT asset classes:

- **Avatars** — identity NFTs.
- **Skins** — cosmetic appearances.
- **Hero Cards** — hero ownership proofs.
- **Plant Flags** — objective cosmetics.

Items carry **tradeability flags**, so the interface only lists assets that are meant to be traded.

## Core marketplace actions

| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Browse / collection** | View listings and your own on-chain collection. |
| **List for sale** | Create a sell order for an NFT you own, at a price you set. |
| **Buy / fulfill** | Purchase a listed NFT, fulfilling the seller's order. |
| **Cancel** | Withdraw a listing you previously created. |
| **History / records** | Review your past transactions and activity. |

Behind the scenes, the game handles order creation, fulfillment, and cancellation against the underlying platforms, and can **estimate gas** and **convert prices** (for example, ETH to USD) so you can make informed decisions.

## In-game marketplace surfaces

The marketplace experience is woven through several in-game screens:

- A **Chain Shop** for browsing and buying on-chain items.
- A **Chain Bag** showing your owned on-chain assets.
- **Sell** and **Listing** screens for putting items on the market.
- A **Collection** view of everything you hold.
- A **Records** view of your trading history.

## Pricing and tokens

Listings are priced in the relevant chain's currency or token. The marketplace recognizes balances across multiple tokens — including **ACP, ETH, IMX, USDT, and FATE** — so trades settle in the appropriate asset for the platform and chain involved.

## Trading and ownership sync

When a trade completes, ownership transfers on-chain, and Debellum updates its synchronized records so the change is reflected in-game immediately. Any **privileges tied to an NFT** (for example, a title's bonus) move with the asset — if you sell it, the privilege follows the new owner. This keeps the in-game experience consistent with true on-chain ownership.

## Fairness reminder

Because the marketplace deals in **cosmetic and identity** NFTs — and because in-match power is capped and server-authoritative — trading lets players express and invest in their collection without buying competitive advantage.
