# What Is Debellum

Debellum: Last Party is a competitive online game built around two pillars: **deep, skill-based combat** and a **player-owned economy**. It is best described as a Web3 MOBA with an additional Battle Royale survival mode.

## The game in one minute

You pick a hero, drop into a match, and fight to win — either by destroying the enemy base in MOBA mode or by being the last team standing in Battle Royale. Every match feeds a set of progression systems that level up your account, your heroes, and your competitive rank. Along the way you collect cosmetics — avatars, skins, hero cards — and the rarest of these can be minted as NFTs that you genuinely own and can trade.

## Two ways to play

| Mode | Objective | Feel |
|------|-----------|------|
| **MOBA** | Push lanes, take objectives, destroy the enemy core. | Coordinated team strategy, lane control, team fights. |
| **Battle Royale** | Survive a shrinking safe zone and outlast every other team. | Scavenge, position, extract, and fight to be last. |

Both modes share the same heroes, the same skill system, and the same deterministic combat engine, so the skills you build transfer between them.

## What makes it Web3

Most of what you do — leveling up, earning ranks, spending soft currency — happens off-chain so it is instant and free. But the items with lasting value are **on-chain NFTs**:

- **Avatars** that represent your identity.
- **Premium and signature skins** crafted from in-game materials.
- **Hero cards** that prove ownership and unlock heroes.
- Other rare, limited cosmetics.

These can be traded on integrated marketplaces, held as collectibles, or used to demonstrate achievement.

## Designed to be fair

A common fear with Web3 games is "pay-to-win." Debellum is built to avoid that:

- In-match power is determined by the deterministic combat engine, not by spending.
- Hero power progression (star ranks, mastery) is **capped** and earned through play.
- The most valuable NFTs are **cosmetic** or **identity** items, not raw power.
- Critical numbers are computed server-side, with anti-cheat and anti-collusion checks.
